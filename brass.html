Instrument group

Brass

Brass players buzz their lips into a mouthpiece. Valves or a slide change the notes. Brass instruments have a distict sound that is easy to recognize.

Trumpet Trombone Tuba French horn

Trumpet

The trumpet is the highest brass instrument in the family. Sound is created by vibrating the lips into a mouthpiece, and different notes are played by pressing down a combination of valves. Trumpets play a prominent role in marches and fanfares. They tend to play melodies as the sound is bright and cuts through the band. It is a great instrument for beginners wanting to play a brass instrument.

How it sounds

Bright and direct, The trumpet makes a clear statement. while the Trumpet is known for being loud and bold, it can play soft and smooth melodies as well.

Trombone

The trombone creates sound simmilarily to the trumpet but uses a slightly bigger mouthpieceThe trombone uses a slide to play different notes. You move the slide in and out to change the pitch. The slide can also smear from one note to the next. That smear is called a glissando, and is a unique sound that the trombone can make.

How it sounds

Bold and powerful, the trombone plays an important role in the low brass section by supporting the bands overall sound.

Tuba

The tuba is the biggest brass instrument and plays the lowest notes. It is the support that the rest of the band depends on. While the Tuba is not an easy instrument for younger players due to it's size, with some help and experience it is a great instrument to transition to.

How it sounds

Deep, round, and wide. The Tuba plays notes that you can feel as well as hear. The tuba lays the foundation for the rest of the band.

French horn

The French horn is a long tube coiled into a circle. The bell is very wide compared to the rest of the instrument. The french horn uses valves simmilar to a trumpet but is a litle more difficult to play than the trumpet. Some players start on trumpet, then switch to the French horn.

How it sounds

Mellow, round, and noble. The French horn plays in the same range as the trumpet but with a warmer sound.
