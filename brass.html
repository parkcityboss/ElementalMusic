Instrument group

Brass

Brass players buzz their lips into a mouthpiece. Valves or a slide change the notes. This is the loud, golden part of the band.

Trumpet Trombone Tuba French horn

Trumpet

The trumpet is the highest brass instrument in the family. It is a tubular instrument made of brass. Sound is created by vibrating the lips into a mouthpiece. Trumpets play the tune in marches, the fanfare before a big moment, and quiet melodies too. A lot of beginners start here because it is light and the notes speak clearly.

How it sounds

Bright and direct, like a voice calling across a gym. Loud notes feel heroic. Quiet notes can be smooth and singing. Several trumpets together make the top of the band sparkle.

Trombone

The trombone uses a slide instead of three valves. You move the slide in and out to change the pitch. The slide can also smear from one note to the next. That smear is called a glissando, and people recognize it right away.

How it sounds

Bold, like a strong low voice singing. In a march it punches the rhythm. In a slow piece it can be warm and round. Low notes are heavy. High notes cut through the band.

Tuba

The tuba is the biggest brass instrument and plays the lowest notes. The tubing wraps around you, and the bell points up. It is the floor the rest of the band stands on. It is not only “oom-pah.” Newer band music gives the tuba real melodies.

How it sounds

Deep, round, and wide. A soft note is a low hum under everyone else. A strong note you can feel as well as hear. When the tuba is in tune, the whole band sounds bigger.

French horn

The French horn is a long tube coiled into a circle. The bell faces back and down, and one hand stays inside the bell. Levers called rotary valves change the notes. It is one of the harder brass instruments, and one of the most beautiful. Some players start on trumpet, then switch to horn.

How it sounds

Mellow, round, and noble. Soft notes feel far away, like a call from across a field. A whole horn section sounds like a choir. Loud notes can blaze without getting as brassy as a trumpet.
