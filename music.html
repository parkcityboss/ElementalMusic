What a band sounds like

Yes, a plain HTML page can embed videos. The player below is YouTube’s. The video file stays on YouTube, which is why this works on free GitHub Pages. Press play. You need an internet connection for the videos to load.

The Stars and Stripes Forever

John Philip Sousa. This is the national march of the United States, played by “The President’s Own” United States Marine Band. It is the most famous concert-band piece there is.

First Suite in E-flat

Gustav Holst. Written for military band in 1909. A lot of people treat this as the piece that turned the concert band into a serious ensemble. Tokyo Kosei Wind Orchestra.

Bugler’s Holiday

Leroy Anderson. Three trumpet soloists trade a bright, flashy tune. This performance is the United States Marine Band. Listen for how the brass can be the star and still sit inside a full band.
