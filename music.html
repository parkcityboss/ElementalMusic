Bands in Action

What a band sounds like

Below are examples of bands playing different pieces.

Here you can watch and listen to how all the different instruments come together to create music.

The Stars and Stripes Forever

John Philip Sousa. This is the national march of the United States, played by “The President’s Own” United States Marine Band. It is the most famous concert-band piece there is.

First Suite in E-flat

Gustav Holst. Written for military band in 1909. A lot of people treat this as the piece that turned the concert band into a serious ensemble. Tokyo Kosei Wind Orchestra.

Summerland

William Grant Still. Performed by the Singapore Wind Symphony. William Grant Still was a famous african american composer who wrote very moving and emotional music. This piece puts more focus on the woodwinds and showcases the lush sounds they can create.
